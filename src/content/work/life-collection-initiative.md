##

For a game jam in 2018. The intended theme of the game jam was to make a bad sequel to an existing property. I had just recently played and finished Mass Effect 3, and my goal was to follow up the ending from that game that I enjoyed the most, wherein organic and inorganic life is combined. Unfortunately, this led me further off track from the game jam's intent - which was more to make funny bad versions of existing game systems than do a bad follow-up narratively - so my submission didn't end up... submitted.

However, it was a fun first try at doing a solo Unity project from the ground up (besides acquired visual and audio assets, as referenced on the project page). Getting used to the engine, engaging with control schemes, front-end components, physics collision types, and really just putting out something with a beginning-to-end flow in a short time span.
